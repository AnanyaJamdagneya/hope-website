==================================================
  HOPE WEBSITE — WHERE TO PUT PHOTOS & VIDEO
==================================================

Just drag image files into these folders (inside the hope-website folder).
You can add as many as you want. JPG or PNG both work.

GALLERIES (show up as collages on each tab)
-------------------------------------------------
  assets/gallery/auction/       -> HOPE Auction photos
  assets/gallery/blooddrives/   -> Blood Drive photos
  assets/gallery/showcase/      -> Service Showcase photos

After you add photos to ANY gallery folder, run this once in Terminal:

    cd ~/hope-website
    python3 gen-gallery.py

That makes the new photos show up. (If you cannot run it, just tell me and I will.)

MAIN PHOTO for a tab (the big one at the top):
  The first photo by name becomes the header. To force one, rename it so it
  sorts first, e.g.  00-main.jpg
  (Tell me which auction photo is the green-dresses group and I will set it.)


BOARD PHOTOS — each exec has a CAROUSEL (multiple photos)
-------------------------------------------------
Each exec has their own folder. Drop as many photos as you want in it,

(01 shows first). Right now each folder has the real headshot + a few
"add a photo" placeholders you can delete/replace.

After adding board photos, run:  python3 gen-gallery.py
